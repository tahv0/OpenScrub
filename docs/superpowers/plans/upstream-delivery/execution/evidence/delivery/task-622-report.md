## Implemented interfaces and lock order

`FileOwners.initialize(job)` continues creating the existing `_JobFiles` state before new jobs or either rehydration path enter `JOBS`. That state now also holds one stable `report_lock`, the current `operation` identity, and an `active` worker owner. `_Lease.operation` binds the same owner to an operation-specific cancellation Event. All synchronization remains private, excluded from persisted options, reports and public job JSON.

The required ordering is preserved:

1. Request/worker lifecycle admission reserves under `VAULT_LOCK` → short `JOBS_LOCK`, then releases both. `_prepare_work(job, action)` obtains the full worker lifetime and, for renders, calls the reviewed `_select_render_input(job, owner)` before taking any report lock. Related-source reservation and provisional release may reacquire `VAULT_LOCK` here.
2. `report_lock` covers admission or latest-read/apply/publication and may take only a short identity/ticket lookup under `JOBS_LOCK`. `_report_current` checks exact live dictionary identity and the existing deleting gate; `_edit_gate` also refuses queued/running/cleaning-up workers. No lifecycle reservation/release, inference, decoding, output hashing or network transfer occurs inside this lock.
3. Leave `report_lock` before releasing lifecycle ownership. `_finish_operation` releases the real worker lifetime before clearing its mutation gate; no file publication remains after that release. Stale/deleted tickets release idempotently without stopping the worker.

`_reserve_queue_locked(job, action, prepared)` now transfers an already prepared lifetime under `report_lock`; it never calls lifecycle reservation or reacquires an already-held `JOBS_LOCK`. It installs operation identity, queued phase and the existing queue generation/ticket before queue submission. Pickup still uses Task621's existing ticket identity and queue ownership; no competing lifetime counter or registry was added. Cancellation extracts unstarted tickets under the report/short-registry boundary and releases them after leaving it. Unstarted cancellation therefore permits immediate retry after acknowledgement; running cancellation retains its active gate through actual cleanup.

Render admission first checks phase/active tracker/work under the job lock, snapshots operation and current input, prepares ownership outside it, then rechecks phase, source and operation before transferring the ticket. A changed preparation returns409 and releases its owner outside the report lock. Tracking similarly validates its prepared source/operation at admission, catching scan normalization that finished while its lifetime preparation waited. Ordinary sparse saves do not change operation identity. The selected input remains privately pinned as `args._report_input`; ordinary engine/CLI/GUI source selection and settings exclusion are unchanged. Skip-review prepares the next render's protected source outside the lock, then validates the scan identity and atomically transfers its next ticket inside it.

The shared allowlist ownership and its four reviewed success/refusal consumer functions remain unchanged. Packaging checks compare the complete APP_JS and the CLI parser, engine source-selection function/settings function, and web `build_args` AST against the exact base.

## Report mutations, worker publication, and readers

- Save: fresh raw report read, current identity/deleting/phase gate, sparse enabled/geometry/time/manual/audio application, atomic publication and response derivation all share `report_lock`. Omitted fields and unknown additive fields remain intact; explicit audio stays whole-list replacement. Missing report returns404; queued/running/cleaning-up scan/render returns409. Parse, application, serialization, fsync and replacement failures cannot return success, and waiting requests proceed after failure.
- Tracking: reserve the existing single-tracker slot while holding the prepared lifecycle owner and job lock. Compute outside it. Final identity/status validation, latest document/FPS/offset read, next integer track ID allocation, row construction, append and publication share one transaction. A normal independent save does not invalidate tracking. Errors release ownership; old attempts cannot replace a newer tracking status.
- Engine: `write_report(path, args, state, output_path=None, publisher=None)` constructs the complete retained P06D document and hashes output before invoking its optional final publisher. Default remains P06A `atomic_report`; existing callbacks without a hook keep default publication. `run_render` passes optional `cb.publish_report`. Web scan and render both use a publisher that locks the job, validates operation identity/Event, then calls the real atomic helper. The engine has no web import or global web registry.
- Cancellation: capture the operation and signal its own Event immediately before waiting for the report lock. At acknowledgement, invalidate only that captured current identity. A delayed old cancel neither signals nor invalidates a newer attempt or its ticket. Later Event clearing cannot revive an invalidated publisher. A report already committing may finish before acknowledgement; cancellation does not undo it. Terminal phase/error/output assignment also validates identity under the same job lock.
- Deletion invalidates operation identity under the job lock after Task621's lifecycle deletion admission has closed the gate; filesystem reap remains outside that lock.
- Report download constructs `send_file` metadata and opens its descriptor under `report_lock`, then transfers after leaving it. Content-Length and body therefore refer to one complete generation. Task621's existing streamed ownership remains active until iterable close. Other report readers parse one already-open atomic generation; detached documents are decoded outside the transaction. No live-preview or thumbnail caching changes were made.
